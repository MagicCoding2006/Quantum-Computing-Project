Asset Lab · Shallow predictive experiment

Can quantum features add a useful market signal?

Exploratory chronological testing on previously inspected historical data. A positive return can reflect market exposure. This experiment does not establish alpha, quantum speedup, or future profit.

Only the two quantum-feature families use the selected feature source. Classical controls keep their original inputs. All model fitting, selection, and backtesting run classically.

Trading assumptions and costs

Long or cash in one asset, no leverage, fractional adjusted-price units, zero interest on cash. A signal at close enters at the following close. The position exits after the fixed holding period, including both transaction costs. Cost scenarios retain the same selected trades. Stops, broker fills, and live account positions are not implemented here.
