RESEARCH · NO BROKER ORDERS

What are we testing?

Buy an asset trading below its fitted peer relationship, only when the market filter permits. Exit on recovery, deterioration, a holding limit, or a scheduled fold boundary.

Walk-forward equity

Signals use a completed close; fills use the next close. Benchmarks buy once at the first evaluated close and sell at the last, including costs. They remain fully invested; the strategy can hold cash, so return differences alone do not establish alpha. Cash earns zero in this model.
